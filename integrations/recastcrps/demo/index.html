What the score means

CRPS measures how closely a forecast distribution matches the eventual observation. Lower is better. Like the wind forecasts, this score is measured in knots.

How the replay works

Forecasts come from NOAA’s ATCF archive. Each model cycle is real. Within a cycle, members arrive in identifier order so you can inspect each edit. Mid-cycle scores mix old and revised members.
