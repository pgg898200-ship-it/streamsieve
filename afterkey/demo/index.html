    What happens with 4,096 readers?

    On a single thread with a million populated keys, p99 catch-up took 1.67 µs for Afterkey and 4.99 µs for a cursor log when writes moved among hot keys. With uniform writes, the log won at 75.92 µs against Afterkey’s 417.86 µs. Each measurement includes delivery to the in-process callback.
